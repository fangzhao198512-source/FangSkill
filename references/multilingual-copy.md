# FangSkill Multilingual Copy

## Principles

- Treat language as a campaign parameter.
- Write for buyers, not for translation exercises.
- Keep on-screen text short enough for vertical mobile ads.
- Use voiceover for detail and captions for high-signal phrases.
- Localize CTA to inquiry behavior: ask for quote, send specs, contact factory.

## Reusable Copy Angles

Energy saving:

- Lower electricity cost with efficient motor selection.
- Choose the right motor for long-term operating savings.
- Send power, voltage, and quantity for an energy-saving quote.

Factory direct:

- Direct factory supply for distributors and projects.
- Stable production, bulk supply, export-ready packaging.
- Send your requirement and get a factory quote.

Durability:

- Built for stable industrial operation.
- Reliable motor supply for factories and equipment builders.
- Match the right model before ordering.

## Language Notes

Turkish:

- Use "enerji tasarruflu motor", "elektrik maliyetinizi dusurun", "teklif alin", "guc, voltaj ve adet".
- Keep Turkish diacritics in final captions when the rendering font supports them.

English:

- Use direct export language: "factory-direct motors", "energy-saving motor supply", "send your specs for a quote".

Arabic:

- Use short lines and verify right-to-left rendering before batch export.
- Prefer human review for important claims.

Spanish:

- Use "motores de bajo consumo", "cotizacion", "envie potencia, voltaje y cantidad".

Russian:

- Use concise buyer language and verify Cyrillic font support before rendering.

## Avoid

- Long feature lists on screen.
- Generic slogans without purchase intent.
- Claims such as guaranteed savings unless the user provides proof.
- Platform-evasion wording. Make distinct creatives by changing footage, copy, pacing, and CTA.
