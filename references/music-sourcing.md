# FangSkill Music Sourcing

## Default Rule

Use existing online/free music tracks for BGM. Do not synthesize background music with generated tones as the default for advertising deliverables.

## Preferred Sources

Prefer sources that allow download and provide clear licensing:

- Pixabay Music: useful for commercial, corporate, product, motivational, technology, and background music searches.
- Mixkit Stock Music: useful for free stock music, intros, upbeat corporate tracks, and short social ads.
- YouTube Audio Library: usable when the project distribution matches the license; verify attribution and off-platform usage requirements before using it for Meta/Facebook ads.

Avoid random YouTube "no copyright" channels unless the license is clear and saved. Do not use copyrighted commercial songs.

## Search Terms

For industrial B2B ads, search with:

- corporate upbeat
- industrial technology
- energetic business
- product promo
- motivational background
- factory technology
- innovation corporate
- powerful percussion

## License Ledger

For each downloaded track, save a small ledger near the project output, such as `_music_ledger.csv`, with:

- file name
- track title
- artist/creator
- source site
- source URL
- license URL or license type
- attribution required: yes/no
- download date
- campaign/video filenames using it

## Audio Mix Targets

Treat these as practical starting points, then listen-check:

- With voiceover: BGM around -20 dB to -16 dB under narration.
- Hook and CTA without dense narration: BGM can rise around -14 dB to -10 dB.
- Use fade-in and fade-out instead of hard starts/stops.
- Duck music under voiceover if the track has strong drums or vocals.
- Avoid vocal-heavy tracks that compete with multilingual narration.

## QC

Before final delivery:

- Verify final videos contain an audio stream.
- Listen to at least three videos in the batch.
- Confirm BGM is clearly audible on phone/laptop speakers.
- Confirm the voiceover remains understandable.
- Confirm the ledger exists when network music is used.
