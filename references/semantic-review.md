# Finished-ad semantic review

Before delivery or an authorized upload, review a segment table: time range, actual visible subject/action, on-screen text, narration, buyer purpose, and result (pass / revise / unverified).

## Decisions that need visual evidence

- Identify the actual product and distinguish component machining, assembly, inspection, performance testing, packing and finished equipment. Do not call machining a test, parts stock a finished-unit inventory, or a stationary unit a running demonstration.
- Captions and narration should describe the visible scene or clearly introduce a separate buyer action. If requesting an old-machine nameplate, prefer a finished blower or a genuine nameplate shot; do not imply a grinding machine is the buyer's blower.
- Evaluate the full sequence, not only attractive individual frames: product recognition, buyer use case, evidence, procurement information, and destination should be coherent. Several consecutive generic machining clips may weaken product/application comprehension.
- Ask only for selection information relevant to the product. State that parameters can be shared if known; do not imply selection is finalized from a photo alone.
- Check claims against supplied evidence. A factory scene alone does not establish certification, quantified savings, guaranteed delivery, durability, testing of every unit, or business ownership.
- Compare source identities with both the usage ledger and older render manifests when the ledger is incomplete. Copies with different filenames can still contain the same footage.

## Actual-output checks

Inspect each segment plus opening/ending and cut boundaries in the rendered file. Source-frame review is not proof that the final crop or subtitles are correct.

Listen to the actual mixed export, including opening, dense speech and CTA, using an available audio-capable tool or human review. Check pronunciation, clipping, timing, intelligibility, BGM masking and unexpected silence. Reading TTS text, measuring loudness, or confirming an audio stream is not listening. If no listening capability is available, mark audio listening unverified, provide the preview and never claim full audiovisual approval.

Record technical, semantic-visual, and audio-listening outcomes separately. Fix observable defects before upload. An upload can remain a review asset with disclosed unresolved checks, but do not activate an ad or claim release-ready status from incomplete review. Authorization to edit/upload media does not authorize budget changes, new active ads, or campaign activation.
