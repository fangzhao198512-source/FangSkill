# FangSkill Ad Video Workflow

## Campaign Brief

Infer missing details from context when the user is continuing an existing task. Otherwise identify:

- Product and buyer: e.g. electric motors for factory buyers, distributors, OEMs, project procurement.
- Market language: Turkish, English, Arabic, Spanish, Russian, or another requested language.
- Main angle: energy saving, factory direct, durability, fast delivery, customization, bulk supply.
- Format: default 9:16, 1080x1920, 20-35 seconds for Facebook/Reels/TikTok.
- Quantity: one test video first when quality is uncertain; batch after approval.

## Ad Structure

Use this sequence unless the user provides a stronger script:

1. Hook: make the product and buyer benefit clear in the first 3 seconds.
2. Product proof: show motor detail, assembly, testing, inventory, packaging, or shipment.
3. Pain point: electricity cost, unstable supply, wrong model selection, downtime, procurement risk.
4. Benefit: energy-saving selection, stable performance, factory supply, model matching, bulk quote.
5. Trust signal: real factory footage, production line, QC, warehouse, delivery, aerial factory view.
6. CTA: ask the buyer to send voltage, power, quantity, and application for quotation.

## Visual Rules

- Avoid opening with an unlabelled factory shot. Overlay a direct benefit hook.
- Prefer real product/factory visuals over abstract AI imagery.
- Do not use source videos with burned-in subtitles unless the user explicitly approves them.
- Keep captions inside safe margins: top and bottom overlays must not be clipped on 9:16.
- Use dynamic background movement through actual footage, zoom/pan, speed ramps, or motion cards.
- Use fewer words per caption; split long localized lines.

## Batch Rules

For a 10-video batch, prepare 10 distinct rows:

- Unique hook
- Unique first shot or crop
- Unique main benefit sentence
- Unique CTA wording
- Different footage order
- Different final factory/product proof shot

The goal is real creative variety, not cosmetic duplication.

## QC Checklist

Before delivery:

- Count final mp4 files.
- Verify 1080x1920 or requested aspect ratio.
- Verify audio stream exists.
- Confirm duration range.
- Extract opening and ending frames from at least three videos.
- Inspect text fit, language, product clarity, CTA, and absence of unwanted burned-in subtitles.
